# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## 1. Problem Identification

Managing a budget and selecting suitable products or services can be difficult for users. Users may have different requirements, budgets and preferences, making it difficult to decide what to choose.

PocketSmart AI aims to provide a simple application that helps users plan their budget and receive suitable recommendations based on their requirements.

## 2. Brainstorming Ideas

The following ideas were considered:

1. Personal Budget Planner
2. Home Interior Budget Planner
3. Party Planning Assistant
4. Jewelry Recommendation Assistant
5. AI-based Product Recommendation System

## 3. Selected Idea

The selected idea is **PocketSmart AI – AI-Powered Smart Budget and Recommendation Assistant**.

The application combines budget planning and AI-based recommendations to help users make suitable choices within their available budget.

## 4. Main Features

- User registration and login
- Home Interior Planner
- Party Planner
- Jewelry Planner
- Budget allocation
- Personalized recommendations
- Recommendation history
- AI-based recommendation support

## 5. Expected Outcome

PocketSmart AI will provide users with a simple way to plan their budget and receive suitable recommendations based on their requirements and preferences.
